# Research — Features

## Hero

Group AI DJ:
- fast taste calibration;
- shared room;
- live reactions;
- explainable next-track choice;
- fairness;
- VibeBridge.

## Complementary platform feature

Smart Lyrics:
- synchronized line highlighting where data exists;
- plain lyric fallback;
- translate/explain;
- lyric moments.

## Retention/shareability

Taste DNA and Room Health make the invisible recommendation model visible and shareable.

## Differentiation

Avoid “AI playlist generator” positioning. The differentiator is **continuous multiplayer adaptation** and fair group coordination.
