# Future Roadmap

## Post-MVP Tier 1
- Taste Match between friends.
- “Between These Songs” discovery.
- Music Memories / saved room snapshots.
- Host handoff.
- Better account-based history.

## Tier 2
- semantic audio/text embeddings;
- personalized long-term taste learning;
- room templates;
- richer multilingual lyrics interpretation.

## Experimental
- headphone synchronization;
- PWA/offline room controls;
- optional local-network direct messaging;
- learned recommendation model after enough consented data.

## Avoid until validated
- public social feed;
- creator marketplace;
- custom streaming catalog;
- phone-speaker synchronization as a headline feature.
